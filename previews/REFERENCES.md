# Grip references

The revised pose was reviewed against police photographs in this [public photo montage](https://github.com/lonlonago/Police-Officer-Detection-Dataset-2105-imagesVOC-YOLO-format/blob/9ec8a9c8e11be052bb59ec03c3915cedb871da42/01.gif):

- `xyr_images_police184.jpg`: hand at the left collar/upper chest, with a low elbow and the hand lying roughly parallel to the chest. Used for the arm and wrist posture.
- `xyr_images_police1163.jpg`: handheld radio near the mouth, with fingers grouped around its side. Used for the compact finger grip.
- `xyr_images_police192.jpg`: rear/side view of an upright handheld-radio grip. Used as a second grip reference.

The montage contains small thumbnails. They show the overall posture and grip, but do not establish individual finger angles, button contact or whether the officer is transmitting. The character sample has no shoulder microphone prop; the animation grips its intended position at the collarbone.

The reference photographs are linked here; the repository previews render the Bohemia sample character.
